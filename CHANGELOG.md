# What's new in Cap Day

Each version of the app is listed here, newest first, with what changed in plain words.
The version you have is shown at the bottom of the app.

How the numbers work, for a version like 0.1.2:

- The last number goes up for a fix (0.1.1 → 0.1.2).
- The middle number goes up when something new is added (0.1.2 → 0.2.0).
- 1.0.0 is reserved for the first app-store release.

Schedules, squads, scores and rankings refresh on their own all day. Those are content updates, not new versions, and are not listed here.

## 0.1.0 — not yet released

The first numbered version of Cap Day: when the U.S. women's and men's national teams play next, what the match is for, and how to watch.

- **Next match for both teams** on the front page, women listed first, opening on whichever team plays soonest.
- **A match card** with a countdown, where to watch, the venue, and three plain lines: what the competition is, what this match is, and what is at stake. A longer section explains how the competition works and how each side got here.
- **Standings** for the competition the match belongs to: the two-leg tie or the group table.
- **Live mode** during a match: score, clock, goals and cards, team stats and lineups, updating about every 30 seconds.
- **Call the score** before kickoff and see how you did at full time.
- **Players:** who is called up, their caps and goals for the USA, and what each did in their club's last three matches. The next opponent's squad gets the same treatment.
- **Calendar:** every fixture tagged by what it is for (friendly, Nations League, qualifier and so on), dates further ahead, and a subscription that adds all matches to your phone's calendar.
- **News** for each team, recent results, stadium details, and FIFA rankings with a win chance worked out from them.
- **Installable** on Android from the browser, with a tip for iPhone. Works offline with the last copy it loaded.
- **The World Cup section:** the complete 2026 tournament, men's and women's world rankings, and every World Cup since 1930. This was the whole app before Cap Day.
- **Privacy:** no accounts and no cookies. Visits are counted anonymously.
