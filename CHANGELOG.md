# What's new in AO Matchday

Each version of the app is listed here, newest first, with what changed in plain words.
The version you have is shown at the bottom of the app.

How the numbers work, for a version like 0.1.2:

- The last number goes up for a fix (0.1.1 → 0.1.2).
- The middle number goes up when something new is added (0.1.2 → 0.2.0).
- 1.0.0 is reserved for the first app-store release.

Schedules, squads, scores and rankings refresh on their own all day. Those are content updates, not new versions, and are not listed here.

## 0.2.0 — not yet released

- **Cap Day is now AO Matchday.** The Cap Day name is retired for now. The old address opens AO Matchday, the World Cup page leads back to it, and the calendars, the privacy page and the picture that shows when the link is shared all carry the new name.
- **AO Matchday is AO Central New Jersey's app.** The version of Cap Day under American Outlaws naming is no longer a concept: the chapter uses it. It shows where the chapter is watching each match, and every other chapter's watch party in the country, nearest first, with a search by city, state or bar. The listings are read from American Outlaws' site twice a day.
- **Watch parties in your calendar.** "Add to calendar" on the AO page subscribes your phone to your chapter's watch parties, with the bar and its address on each one.
- **A sign for the bar.** A page to print and put up, with a QR code that opens the app.
- **A link and a sign for every chapter.** "Share this chapter's link" in Find your crew gives a link that opens the app on that chapter, and "Print a sign for the bar" prints that chapter's own sign and QR code.
- **A note from your chapter.** Chapter leaders can write a line for the watch party card ("Doors at 6, upstairs") and it appears in the app, for one match or for all of them.
- **Matches you can drive to.** A home match within about 250 miles of your chapter is flagged in the calendar and on the watch party card.
- **Songbook.** The chapter's chants and songs, once its leaders have written them down.
- **Reminders with your watch parties.** A subscribed chapter calendar now reminds you the day before and two hours before kickoff, where your calendar app allows it.
- **AO Matchday can be found in search.** The page is no longer hidden from search engines, and its title spells out American Outlaws Central New Jersey.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-15

The first numbered version of Cap Day: when the U.S. women's and men's national teams play next, what the match is for, and how to watch.

- **A goal that is taken away no longer looks like a goal.** When a goal is ruled out after review the score goes back down. The match card used to flash and buzz for that as if someone had scored. Now it stays still and shows a short note, "Goal ruled out. The score has been corrected."
- **A shorter page.** Everything below the match card (storylines, news, players, the opponent, the calendar, results) now folds to one line with a preview of what is inside. Tap to open; the app remembers what you left open.
- **Install, when the phone doesn't offer it.** On Android the Install button now always appears and, when Chrome has no prompt to give, shows the three steps to do it by hand.
- **Next match for both teams** on the front page, women listed first, opening on whichever team plays soonest.
- **A match card** with a countdown, where to watch, the venue, and three plain lines: what the competition is, what this match is, and what is at stake. A longer section explains how the competition works and how each side got here.
- **Standings** for the competition the match belongs to: the two-leg tie or the group table.
- **Live mode** during a match: score, clock, goals and cards, team stats and lineups, updating about every 30 seconds. Half time is shown as half time.
- **Call the score** before kickoff and see how you did at full time. **Challenge a friend:** share your call as a link or a QR code; they see it, make their own, and the calls are ranked during the match and at full time. Nothing goes through a server.
- **Players:** who is called up, their caps and goals for the USA, and what each did in their club's last three matches. The next opponent's squad gets the same treatment.
- **Calendar:** every fixture tagged by what it is for (friendly, Nations League, qualifier and so on), dates further ahead, and a subscription that adds all matches to your phone's calendar.
- **News** for each team, recent results, stadium details, and FIFA rankings with a win chance worked out from them.
- **Did you know?** One short fact about international soccer at a time: some explain how it works for people new to it, some are deep cuts for people who follow it. Facts about the next opponent come first.
- **Installable** on Android from the browser, with a tip for iPhone. Works offline with the last copy it loaded.
- **The World Cup section:** the complete 2026 tournament, men's and women's world rankings, and every World Cup since 1930. This was the whole app before Cap Day.
- **Honest about its own state:** it says so when its schedule information is more than a day or so old, when it cannot load, and when a newer version is ready to refresh to.
- **Privacy:** no accounts and no cookies. Visits are counted anonymously.
