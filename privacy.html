Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Cap Day is an unofficial fan app for following the U.S. women's and men's national soccer teams. It is built to work without knowing who you are.

What Cap Day does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in, names, email addresses or phone numbers.
  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No cookies.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera or files.

What stays on your device

Your score predictions, your answers to the feedback question and a few display choices are saved in your browser's storage on your own phone or computer. They are never sent anywhere. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes them.

Anonymous visit counts

Cap Day uses GoatCounter to count visits and a few actions (for example, that a score was locked in, a feedback button was tapped, or part of the page failed to load). GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store your IP address or anything that identifies you. It records the page or action, the referring site, and general browser and screen-size information, and reports them as totals.

Requests to other services

To show its content, your browser fetches data directly from other services. As with any website, those services receive your IP address and browser details when it does:

  • ESPN: live scores, lineups, news headlines, and team and club logos.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia: stadium photographs.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app itself.

Links to news stories, Wikipedia and calendar services open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Children

Cap Day collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it. Questions can be raised through the project's page at github.com/jerredcook/wc2026.

Cap Day is not affiliated with or endorsed by U.S. Soccer, Concacaf or FIFA.
